Published work

Nordic Council of Ministers · Systemiq · 2023

Towards Ending Plastic Pollution by 2040

TemaNord 2023:539

A report commissioned by the Nordic Council of Ministers and developed by Systemiq, setting out 15 global policy interventions for the international legally binding instrument on plastic pollution. If universally adopted, the modelled scenario cuts annual mismanaged plastic waste by 90% and virgin plastic use by 30% by 2040, against 2019 levels.

I contributed as part of the Systemiq team while a working student, researching plastics use in automotive applications and in paints and coatings, and carrying out supporting calculations. I am credited among the Systemiq reviewers and contributors.
